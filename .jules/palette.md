## 2025-05-14 - [Accessibility & Build Reliability]
**Learning:** Next.js build process (SWC) fails when template literals are backslash-escaped (e.g., `\${var}`), producing "Expected unicode escape" errors. Additionally, standard accessibility practices like `aria-label` and `sr-only` labels are critical for icon-only interfaces.
**Action:** Always verify template literals are not escaped and ensure interactive elements have clear, accessible descriptors.
